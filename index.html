Questions teachers ask

Do I need any experience with AI?

No. We start from the very beginning and work through every exercise together.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no fee, and you won’t need to buy anything or enter payment details.

Which subjects and grades is it for?

All of them. Examples cover primary and secondary classes, and you’ll practise with your own subject.

What should I bring?

A laptop or tablet with internet, and one lesson or task you’d like to improve.

Will I get the materials afterwards?

Yes. Everyone who registers receives the slides and prompt examples by email.
